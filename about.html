Our Story

Sanjay Telecom began as a small retail outlet with a big vision: to make premium telecom services accessible to every doorstep. As an authorized Airtel distributor, we have grown into a trusted name, serving 1,200+ customers with SIM activation, broadband, DTH, and corporate solutions. Our journey is defined by reliability, speed, and a customer-first approach.
